# Point 3: dial bands (proposal, awaiting the owner's approval)

Date: 25 November 2026. Nothing here is built yet.

One colour rule for every dial: **green = supports buying crypto, amber = neutral, red = argues against.**
"Now" is the reading on 24–25 November 2026 from the point 2 source check.

## Rules that apply to every dial

- A dial changes colour only after **two runs in a row** beyond a band edge.
- Price levels carry a **2% buffer**: Bitcoin must be 2% beyond a level to change the colour (for example, the price dial turns green at $57,430, not $58,600, and back to amber at $59,770).
- The dollar index changes a dial only after **two ECB fixings in a row** at least **0.3** beyond a level (owner's decision, point 2).
- Monthly and weekly series (margin debt, CPI, Fed balance sheet) change only when a new figure is published.
- If a source fails, the dial keeps its last colour, greyed out.
